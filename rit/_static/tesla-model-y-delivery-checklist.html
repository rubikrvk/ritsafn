Delivery Checklist

A walk-through for taking delivery of your 2026 Model Y in Iceland — written for a first-time owner, with how-to notes for every check.

Fri 5 June 2026 · 11:00 Reykjavík · Vatnagarðar Premium LR · AWD Quicksilver · 19″ Crossflow Black interior · 5-seat
The one rule that matters most

Don't tap "Accept Delivery" until you're happy.

You inspect the car before you press Accept Delivery in the Tesla app. Once you accept, the car is yours and getting issues fixed becomes slower and less flexible.

For anything you find — a scratch, an uneven gap, a loose trim piece — take photos and log it on-site before you leave. You're also allowed to reject the car if something is seriously wrong. Take your time; the staff are there to help, and no question is too basic.

01Before you go — paperwork & app

Sort these out the night before or first thing, so nothing holds up the handover.

Active insurance in place
In Iceland you must at least have mandatory liability cover (ábyrgðartrygging) active before you drive away. Confirm with your insurer that the policy is live from today and covers this car's VIN, and keep proof on your phone.

02Exterior walkaround

Ask the specialist to unlock the car so you can inspect it before accepting. Do this in good light — and use your phone's torch held at a low, shallow angle to catch flaws in the paint.

VIN on the windscreen
There's a small VIN plate at the base of the windscreen on the driver's side. Confirm it matches your paperwork.
Paint condition (Quicksilver)
Walk slowly all the way around. Look for scratches, chips, dull patches, swirl marks or specks trapped under the clear coat. Quicksilver is a lighter metallic, so pay attention where light reflects off the panels.
Before you sign
Panel gaps — even & symmetrical
Look at the gaps around the doors, the front boot (frunk) and the rear hatch. Each gap should be roughly the same width along its length, and match the same gap on the opposite side of the car. Large or uneven gaps are the single most common Tesla build issue.
Before you sign
Doors sit flush with the body
Stand at each rear corner and sight along the side of the car toward the front. No door should stick out past the bodywork or sink in. A door that visibly sticks out ("proud") is worth flagging before you sign — it's not always fixable afterwards.
Before you sign
Front light bar & headlights
Your car has the full-width light strip across the front. Check the gaps between the lights and the body are even, and look inside the lenses for any moisture or condensation.
Rear light bar & tail-lights
Check the gap around each tail-light is equal on the left and right (one side shouldn't be visibly wider than the other), and that there's no condensation inside the lenses.
Rear "TESLA" trim strip
Gently press the top and bottom edges of the black strip running across the hatch. It should be firmly attached, not lifting, and shouldn't creak or squeak.
Wing mirrors
Lightly squeeze the black plastic arm of each mirror — it shouldn't creak or feel loose. Check the mirror glass for cracks.
Black window & door trim
Run your eye and a finger along the black trim around the windows and doors. It should be evenly fitted, fully attached, and free of scratches or discolouration.
Exterior cameras
There are small camera housings on the front wings, above the front grille, and near the hatch button. Give each a very gentle wiggle — they should be firmly seated, not loose.
Wheels & tyres (19″ Crossflow)
Check each of the four alloy wheels for scuffs, scratches or "curb rash" on the rim edges. Scan the tyres for any stuck screws, nails or damage. (Note which tyres are fitted — see the winter-tyre item in section 06.)
Your config
Wheel-arch liners
Look up inside each wheel arch. The plastic liner shouldn't sag, and the push-pins holding it in place should be fully seated.
Lower bumpers & underside
Look along the bottom of the front and rear bumpers and the side sills for scrapes, scuffs, or anything hanging down underneath.
Charge-port flap (rear left)
Check the gap around the flap is even all the way round. You'll test that it opens electrically in the next section.
Door handles sit flush
The new Model Y's handles sit almost flush with the body. Check each one is level — not sunken in or popped out — and feels solid when you use it.
03Functions — car unlocked & on

A few of these need the car switched on; ask the specialist to help and to confirm it's fine to test before acceptance.

Test every exterior light
With the staff's help, run through: low and high beam, the front light bar, indicators front and rear, brake lights, reversing light, and the side indicators on the mirror housings.
Wipers & washer fluid
Run the wipers through their speeds. Use the wash/clean button on the left steering stalk to spray washer fluid and confirm it sprays evenly across the glass.
Open & close every door
Each door should open and shut smoothly with a solid, even sound — no rattles or grinding. Test both the outside button and the inside handle on all four doors.
Door rubber seals
Look around each door's rubber seal for bubbles, gaps, or rubber that's lifting away from the frame.
Inner door panels & speakers
With a door open, gently pull up on the inside grab handle — the panel should be firmly attached, not loose. Check the trim and the speaker grille for marks, dents or rips.
All four windows up & down
Lower and raise each window fully, with the door both open and closed. The front windows have one-touch up/down. The windows should also dip slightly on their own when you open a door and re-seal when you close it.
Glass clarity & distortion
Sit inside and look out through the windscreen, the large glass roof, and each window. Check for cracks, chips and scratches — and for any wavy, "fun-house mirror" distortion in the glass.
Powered tailgate
Open and close the rear hatch from the outside button (under the trim strip) and the inside button. It should glide smoothly — not slam or make a loud "pop". Watch that the open hatch doesn't rub against the paint at the rear corners, and that the seals sit evenly on both sides.
Charge-port opens
Press the rear edge of the charge-port flap (or open it from the screen). It should pop open and, a little later, close on its own. This confirms you'll be able to plug in.
04Interior
Pillar & headliner trim (black)
Check the trim panels along the windscreen pillars, the door pillars, and the roof lining for marks, scratches and gaps. Press them gently to confirm they're firmly fixed and don't creak.
Your config
Front seats move fully
Slide each front seat fully forward and back, up and down, and recline it — listen for any grinding. Check the seat material for scratches, deep creases or blemishes.
Every seatbelt
Pull each belt fully out and clip it into its buckle — front and rear. They should latch securely and retract smoothly.
Rear seats & power folding
Check the rear seats sit flush and level. Fold them using the buttons on the seats and again using the buttons in the boot — they should drop and return fully. Lift the rear seat bases to confirm they're secured, and tug the seat-back pockets to check they're attached.
Centre console
Open and close both armrest compartments — they should move smoothly with no creaks. Press the sides of the console to check it's solid. (Tip: record a short video of the front cubby as it closes to confirm its light switches off.)
Wireless phone chargers
Place your phone on each of the two charging pads under the screen and confirm both start charging. You may need to remove a thick case.
Floor mats present
Confirm carpet floor mats are included for both the front and rear.
Sun visors
Fold both front visors down; check the vanity mirror, its light, and that the visors move without odd noises.
Coat hooks & cabin lights
Press the rear coat hooks (they should move smoothly and close properly) and test the interior dome lights on and off.
Glovebox + the Tesla SSD
The glovebox opens via the screen: tap the car icon → Controls → Glovebox. It should open smoothly with a working light. Inside, look for a small Tesla USB stick (SSD) — it's used to store Sentry Mode and dashcam footage.
Steering wheel & horn
Check the wheel rim for marks or tears, and press the centre to test the horn.
Rear-view mirror
It should feel secure and move without rattling.
Indicator stalk
The new Model Y has a proper indicator stalk again — push down for the left indicator, up for the right. As you signal, the relevant side-camera view should pop up on the screen.
05Screen & software

In a Tesla, the central touchscreen controls almost everything — climate, gears, mirrors, lights. Take your time here.

Screen condition & mounting
Check the central screen for scratches, marks or discolouration. Place a flat palm on each side and confirm it's firmly mounted, with no wobble.
Dead-pixel & "phantom touch" test
Open Toybox → Sketchpad and leave a blank drawing on screen for a minute or two. Watch closely: no lines should appear on their own (those are "phantom touches"), and there should be no stuck/dead pixels or discoloured patches.
Before you sign
No error messages / not in transport mode
Check there are no warning messages (usually a yellow box in the lower-left). Confirm the car isn't still in "Transport Mode" (a factory setting that limits speed) — if it is, ask the staff to take it out.
Menus feel responsive
Tap through the various menus — everything should feel quick and smooth, not laggy or stuttering.
Heating & cooling
Open the climate controls (tap the temperature, bottom-left). Turn the fan to maximum and confirm it blows genuinely cold on A/C, then switches to genuinely hot on heat. Try changing the vent direction and the recirculation setting.
Heated AND ventilated seats
Worth checking carefully in Iceland. In the climate menu, turn the heated seats on (and off) for both driver and passenger, then switch to the cooled/ventilated setting and confirm that works too.
Iceland
Heated steering wheel
From the climate menu, turn on the heated steering wheel and confirm the rim warms up. You'll use this a lot through Icelandic winters.
Iceland
Powered steering-wheel adjust
Car icon → Controls → Steering. Move the wheel fully in/out and up/down using the left scroll button on the wheel — it should move smoothly and not creak.
Powered mirror adjust & fold
Car icon → Controls → Mirrors. Adjust each mirror using the left scroll button, then test the fold/unfold function.
Camera feeds
Open the camera view on the screen and confirm clear, sharp images from the front, rear and side cameras — no black screens or blur.
Drive / Reverse / Park (with staff)
With your foot on the brake, use the on-screen swipe selector (and the backup controls in the roof console) to select Drive, Reverse, Neutral and Park. This confirms the new screen-based gear selector works. Only do this with the staff's go-ahead.
Navigation
Type any destination into the map search. It should calculate a route and estimate the battery % you'll arrive with.
Sound system & every speaker
Play some audio (radio or an app). Open the audio settings (the bars icon), nudge the bass and treble, then use the "balance" control to drag the sound to each corner of the cabin — this lets you confirm every speaker works without crackle or rattle.
VIN shown on screen
Car icon → Software. Confirm the VIN displayed matches your paperwork and the windscreen.
Ambient lighting
Car icon → Lights → Accent Lights. Try changing the colours and brightness to confirm the interior light strip works.
Autopilot is present
Open the Autopilot settings on the screen and confirm the Autopilot features you paid for are listed and switched on. (Actually trying them out safely happens later, on the road.)

06What's included + winter tyres
Two key cards (test one)
You should receive two card "keys" in a small sleeve. Test one: tap it just below the driver's-side door-pillar camera to lock/unlock, and place it on the console behind the cupholders to confirm it lets the car be driven.
Phone set up as a key
Have the specialist help you pair your phone so the car unlocks as you approach (through the Tesla app over Bluetooth). This becomes your everyday "key".
Charging equipment (Europe = CCS2 / Type 2)
Your European car uses a CCS2 / Type 2 charge port — the same standard as Icelandic public chargers and Tesla Superchargers here, so there's no US-style "J1772 adapter". Check the boot / rear sub-trunk for the included charging gear — typically a Tesla Mobile Connector for a normal 230 V wall socket — and ask whether a Type 2 cable (for public AC chargers or a home wallbox) is included, since Tesla's inclusions vary. Inspect any cable for damage.

Number plates — front & rear
Iceland requires plates on both the front and the rear. Confirm both plates are fitted, or that you've been given the black front plate holder to mount it.
Iceland
Tow hitch — only if you ordered it
If a tow hitch was part of your order, uncover the panel and confirm it's installed and not loose. (Skip if you didn't order one.)
Your Nokian studded winter tyres — read this carefully
Studded tyres (nagladekk) are only legal in Iceland from 1 November to 15 April — driving on them now (June) risks a fine of about 20,000 ISK per tyre. So your car will be handed over on its standard (non-studded) tyres, and the 19″ Nokian studded set is a second set you keep for winter. Before you leave: (1) confirm which tyres are actually fitted to the car today; (2) confirm you receive the Nokian set, and ask where it is (loose, boxed, or on a separate set of rims); (3) check they're 19″ and that every stud is present and undamaged; (4) ask Tesla how/where the set will be stored and how to book the seasonal swap before 1 November.

07Accepting (or rejecting)

This is the decision point. Everything above happens before you reach it.

Only tap "Accept Delivery" when you're happy
The "Accept Delivery" button is in the Tesla app. The moment you tap it, the car is yours and fixing issues becomes slower and less flexible. Don't rush this.
Before you sign
Photograph & log any issues on-site
For anything you found — a scratch, an uneven gap, a loose trim piece — take clear photos and open a service request in the app (Menu → Service), noting "found on delivery". Doing this before you leave protects you, so Tesla can't later claim you caused the damage on the way home.
Before you sign
You're allowed to reject it
If there are problems you're not willing to live with, tell the delivery team you want to reject the car before tapping Accept. Don't let anyone pressure you into accepting a car you're unhappy with — some issues (like a misaligned door) can't easily be fixed afterwards.
Ask anything — that's what they're for
As a first-time Tesla owner, it's completely normal to ask the specialist to show you how things work — the key card, the screen, charging, getting in and out of Drive. There are no silly questions.
08After you drive away (same day)
Charge it soon — easy win
There's a Tesla Supercharger right at the Vatnagarðar store, plus others in Reykjavík (e.g. Fossvogur and Álfabakki). A 10–15 minute stop confirms fast-charging works — check the charge port glows green while charging.
Iceland
Listen for rattles & wind noise
On a quiet stretch of road, with the windows up and audio off, listen for rattles, squeaks, or excessive wind noise. A light "clunk" from the suspension over bumps is normal; persistent squeaks are not.
Does it drive straight?
On a flat, straight road the car should track straight with the steering wheel centred — no pulling to one side and no shaking through the wheel.
Brakes
When it's safe, brake firmly once or twice and check the car stops smoothly and quietly, with no grinding or pulling.
Premium Connectivity
New cars include 30 days of Premium Connectivity (it works in Iceland). Try streaming media or live traffic to confirm it's active.
USB-C ports & 12 V socket
Plug your phone into the USB-C ports (one in the console cubby, two below the rear screen) to confirm they charge. Test the 12 V socket in the front console cubby if you'll use it.
Re-check lights in the dark
Confirm all exterior lights, the interior dome lights, footwell lights and boot lights work, and that the adaptive high-beam dips for oncoming cars. Tip: in June, Iceland barely gets dark — use a garage to test, or just confirm each light comes on, and do a proper night-time check once the evenings darken.
Report anything new quickly
If you notice something on the drive home, add it to your service request promptly — Tesla generally expects build/quality issues to be reported within about 24 hours of delivery.
✓ Every item checked. Enjoy your new Model Y — and drive safe.

A note on sources

Tesla doesn't publish its own customer-facing inspection checklist — their official delivery pages only cover paperwork and the app flow. This checklist is a synthesis of the most thorough and recent owner and community guides (including Tesla Motors Club, EV Help Hub's 2026 Juniper walk-through, and Tesla Model Guy), tailored to your exact configuration and adapted for Iceland (studded-tyre law, the European CCS2 charge port, front-and-rear plates, and the Reykjavík Tesla store and Superchargers).

It's a guide, not a guarantee — when in doubt on the day, ask the Tesla delivery specialist directly. Your progress is saved on this device automatically.
